The AI review mostly confirmed requirements that were already grounded in the client brief, such as receptionists creating appointments, GPs viewing schedules and cancelled appointments being retained in history. It also flagged several items that had no supporting evidence, including facial recognition login, online payment and AI treatment recommendations. These were unnessacary since none appear anywhere in the SmartCare brief and each would add onto either the scope, cost or risk that the client did not request. The one item that changed after review was the handling of cancelled apointments, the AI emphasised the need to distinguish between soft-deletion and hard-deletion, so that cancellation changes status rather than just removing data. CoPilot also highlighted a missing clarification question about appointment statuses beyond "booked" and "cancelled", which was added to the open questions. This reinforced that requirements must have evidence because a system is built to serve real stakeholders and their actual constraints.
